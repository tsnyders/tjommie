Motion

Calm, weighty, sculptural. Things move like stone that has been set down well: big surfaces arrive on a critically damped spring and settle without a bounce; small parts land with a short settle. Every motion starts on the frame of the input, can be caught and turned mid-flight, never plays for keyboard actions, and under reduced motion becomes a cross-fade or a snap. Use the bar at the top for reduced motion and the dark theme; each card replays on its own.

Agents feel alive

The eyes are their own layer. Each agent blinks on its own clock, a working agent's eyes narrow and scan instead of a spinner, an agent that needs you looks up, a limited agent is drowsy, a paused agent rests. Type below and Forge glances at what you are writing.

blink: close 70 ms, open 110 ms, every 2.6 to 6.4 s, sometimes twice. scan: 1.8 s each way, ease in-out. glance: snappy spring, 1.8 units of the 40 box, held 1.2 s. Reduced motion: no blinks, scans or glances; each state keeps its still pose.
