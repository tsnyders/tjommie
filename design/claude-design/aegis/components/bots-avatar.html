Rules

Waiting is the ochre dot; working is the eyesA bot that needs you gets the marker dot, the same ochre dot used in Recents and the composer hint, and its eyes look up at you. A working bot gets no badge: its eyes narrow and scan. Limited, paused and needs setup keep a graphite glyph on a raised disc.
20 has no badgeAt 20 the avatar sits beside text that already says the state (a Recents marker, a message header, an exchange line).
The ring is the surfaceThe badge's ring takes the colour of whatever is behind the avatar (panel, raised card or frame), so it reads as cut out, never as an outline.
