Colour

Monochrome stone chrome. The agents are the colour, plus one ochre accent kept for Needs you and unread. Light is the measured Aegis brand; dark is its warm graphite derivation. Ink and paper swap jobs between themes, so the primary pill is near-black by day and paper by night.

Agent colours, shared by both themes

Ten mineral pigments in one lightness band, so seven agents side by side read as a family and none shouts. Each clears 3:1 on paper and on graphite, and keeps its white eyes at 3:1. Amber is the one exception, approved by the owner: it stays light so it is never mistaken for the ochre Needs you accent, and an amber avatar always appears with its agent's name.
