Row heights: 24 dense, 28 default, 44 fleet rows, 52 roster rows. Controls: 24, 28, 32 on desktop; 48 dp on the phone. Desktop icon buttons are never smaller than 28 px.

Radius: tight

xs 2: lamps, pills, key caps
sm 4: controls, rows
md 6: cards, menus
lg 8: dialogs, previews
xl 12: phone sheet top

Elevation: only for things that float

Rows, cards and panels sit flat and are separated by hairlines. Shadows appear only on layers above the window: menus, the palette, dialogs, sheets and toasts.
