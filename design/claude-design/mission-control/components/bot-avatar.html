Bot avatar

The eight shapes and their eyes are the app's own paths, unchanged: a 40 by 40 box, the body filled with the bot colour, two white eye strokes. Mission Control adds only the state badge: a square lamp cut out of the avatar by a ring in the colour behind it.

State badges

Below 40 the badge is a plain lamp; at 40 and 64 it carries the state glyph. Idle has no badge. The badge is never the only signal: rows and headers also carry the state pill or glyph and its words.
